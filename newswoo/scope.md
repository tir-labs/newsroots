1. Physical Shipping & FulfillmentNewsrooms rarely ship physical goods. You can remove the entire logistics and shipping infrastructure.Shipping Zones & Classes: Core settings and database tables tracking geographic shipping rules.Shipping Methods & Calculators: Flat rate, free shipping, and local pickup logic, along with the shipping calculator on the cart page.Weight & Dimensions: Product metadata fields used to calculate freight costs.Shipping Address Fields: Form inputs on the checkout page (e.g., shipping street, apartment, city, zip code).2. Physical Inventory & WarehousingNews sites sell infinite digital access, not limited stock in a warehouse.Stock Management: The logic that increments/decrements inventory counts, manages "backorders," and triggers "low stock" email notifications.Product Dimensions: Data fields and front-end displays for item size and weight.3. Traditional Retail Checkout ElementsA reader buying a subscription expects a sleek, 1-click paywall experience, not a retail shopping trip."Add to Cart" Fragments: The AJAX functionality that updates a shopping cart icon in the header. News sites usually benefit from a direct "Buy Now" link that skips the cart entirely.Cart Page: You can completely eliminate the [woocommerce_cart] page and send users directly from a paywall button to a unified checkout screen.Coupons System: While you might want to keep basic discount codes for promotional subscriptions, you can strip out complex retail logic like "free shipping coupons" or product-specific combination exclusions.4. B2C Retail Product TypesWooCommerce comes built-in with product types that clutter a publisher's workflow.Grouped & External/Affiliate Products: Core product post-type definitions that don't align with digital subscriptions.Complex Attributes/Variations: While subscription tiers exist, you don't need complex matrix variations (like choosing a T-shirt Size and Color). You can replace this with a simplified "Subscription Plan" selector.5. Tax & Geo-location OverheadsWhile digital sales tax (like VAT) exists, you do not need retail-heavy tax engines.Shipping-Based Tax Calculations: Logic that calculates tax based on where a physical item lands.Geo-location for Shipping: Disabling default core scripts that look up user locations just to estimate freight tax before checkout.Comparison: Standard WooCommerce vs. Newsroom EditionFeature / SystemStandard WooCommerceYour Newsroom ForkPrimary ProductPhysical or Digital GoodsSubscriptions, Paywall Access, DonationsCheckout FlowCart → Shipping → Billing → PayDirect Paywall → Minimal Billing/PaymentCore DatabaseHeavy tables for stock, logs, shippingLightweight tables focused on user meta & accessTax BaseShipping address destinationBilling address / Country residency onlyWhat You Should Keep (or Enhance)To make your fork truly valuable to publishers, preserve and optimize these elements:Customer Accounts / My Account: Readers need a dashboard to update their credit cards, view invoices, and manage active subscriptions.Payment Gateway API: Keep the core structural support for Stripe, PayPal, and Apple Pay/Google Pay.Webhooks & REST API: Vital for connecting the news site to third-party email newsletters (like Mailchimp or ActiveCampaign) and CRM systems.
